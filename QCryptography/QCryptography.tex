        \item<+-> The Sender writes their 6-bit \alert{key} on both the Sender and Receiver copies.
        \item<+-> The Sender gives the Receiver copy to the Receiver.
        \item<+-> The Sender chooses a number, converts it to binary, and encrypts it using their key.
        \item<+-> Protocol: bitwise addition (0+0=0, 0+1=1+0=1 and 1+1=0)
        \item<+-> The Sender gives the encrypted message (cipher) to the Receiver.
        \item<+-> The Receiver uses the key to decrypt the message.
    \end{enumerate}

    \begin{enumerate}
        \item<3-> The Sender randomly chooses a bit (0 or 1) to send to the Receiver. Record the 16-bit sequence in the sheet.
        \item<4-> For each bit, the Sender randomly chooses a basis and prepares the corresponding photon state. Record the basis in the sheet.
        \item<5-> The Receiver randomly chooses a basis in which to measure it. Record the basis in the sheet.
        \item<6-> The Sender prepares the photon in the chosen state and sends it to the Receiver.
        \item<7-> The Receiver measures the photon using their chosen basis.
        \item<8-> The Receiver records the bit obtained from the measurement.
        \item<10-> For each bit, the Sender tells the Receiver which basis was used to prepare the photon, without revealing the bit value.
        \item<11-> The Receiver compares this with the basis used for the measurement: If the bases match, keep the bit. If the bases differ, discard the bit. The Receiver tells the Sender which bits to keep and which to discard.
    \end{enumerate}
